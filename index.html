I'm a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Amsterdam with a strong interest in the conceptual and often overlooked questions in AI. Alongside my academic work, I currently hold a part-time role focused on making AI more approachable to my colleagues. My main motivation, however, lies in understanding how and why AI systems behave the way they do—and where they might be pushed in new directions.
